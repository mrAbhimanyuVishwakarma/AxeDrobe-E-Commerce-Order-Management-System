Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information AxeDrobe ("we", "us") collects when you use our online store at https://axedrobe.vercel.app, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have. By using the store you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and/or mobile number, and a password if you choose to set one. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Delivery details: the recipient name, phone number and delivery address you enter at checkout.
  • Orders: the products, sizes, quantities, prices and status of the orders you place.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form or by email.

Information from Sign in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares the following with us, with your permission:

  • your name,
  • your email address and whether Google has verified it,
  • your unique Google account identifier.

We use this information only to create your AxeDrobe account, sign you in and contact you about your orders. We do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive, calendar or any other Google data, and we never receive your Google password.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, device and browser type and approximate location, collected through Google Analytics to understand how the store is used.
  • Browser storage: we store your sign-in token, bag contents, theme preference and last used delivery address in your browser's local storage so the site works as expected. You can clear this at any time from your browser settings.

We do not collect card or bank details. All orders are currently paid by Cash on Delivery.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, including sending one-time sign-in codes by email or SMS.
  • To process, deliver, track and support your orders, and to handle cancellations and returns.
  • To send you service emails such as order confirmations and cancellation notices.
  • To keep the store secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.
  • To understand how the store is used and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Google user data

AxeDrobe's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received from Google is used only to provide sign-in and account features described in this policy, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with third parties except the service providers listed below that host and operate the store for us.

4. Who we share information with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the store, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Hosting and database: Vercel (website), Render (application servers), MongoDB Atlas (database) and Aiven (order event processing).
  • Email and SMS delivery: Brevo for emails, and 2Factor or Fast2SMS for SMS sign-in codes.
  • Sign-in and analytics: Google (Sign in with Google and Google Analytics).
  • Delivery partners: the courier delivering your order receives your name, phone number and delivery address.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and property of our customers or AxeDrobe.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account information is kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.
  • One-time sign-in codes are stored only as a hash and are deleted automatically within an hour.
  • Order records are kept for as long as required for accounting, tax and warranty purposes.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between your browser, our servers and our database. Passwords and one-time codes are stored as one-way hashes. Access to production systems is limited to the people who operate the store. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see your orders in My Orders and ask us to correct your details.
  • Deletion: email us from your registered address to delete your account and personal data.
  • Google access: you can remove AxeDrobe's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
  • Emails: we only send emails related to your account and orders.

We respond to requests within 30 days.

8. Children

The store is not intended for children under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email axedrobe@gmail.com or write to AxeDrobe, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
